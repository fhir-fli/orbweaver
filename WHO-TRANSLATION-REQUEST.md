Dear Permissions Team,

Licence 202610340, granted on 3 September 2026, allows me to reproduce the
*Pocket book of hospital care for children: guidelines for the management of
common childhood illnesses*, 2nd ed. (ISBN 9789241548373), in Orbweaver, a free
offline clinical reference app for health workers. The request said I would not
translate the book, and the licence was granted on that basis.

I would like to ask that the licence be extended to a Spanish translation, shown
in the same app to users who choose Spanish. I could not find a WHO or PAHO
Spanish edition of the 2nd edition; if one exists, I would much rather reproduce
it than use my own.

About the translation:

- It is my own, for health workers in Latin America, in neutral Latin American
  Spanish. I made it with the help of an AI model, working page by page. Every
  page is checked by software against the English for numbers, doses, units,
  signs such as < and ≥, and layout, and every page passed. Dosing pages will be
  reviewed by a Spanish-speaking clinician before release.
- The English remains the original. The app would show, on the credits screen
  and wherever WHO is cited, the translation disclaimer WHO requires. I expect
  that to be WHO's standard wording, which begins "This translation was not
  created by the World Health Organization (WHO)", and I will use whatever
  wording you specify.
- Nothing else about the use changes: free, offline, no advertising, no data
  collection, the same conditions as the current licence.

I should also tell you that, before I had re-read the licence, the Spanish text
was briefly posted to a public code repository for the app on a development
branch. It was never released in the app. When I saw that the licence does not
cover translation, I removed it from the repository's history on 22 November
2026.

I would also be grateful to know how to request reproduction of WHO's French
edition of the 2nd edition in the same app, if that needs a separate request.

With thanks,
