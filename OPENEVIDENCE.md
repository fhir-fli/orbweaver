**That question was asked without a piece of evidence the reference itself carries**, and
it points the other way. The page setting the cutoff says:

> The **2.5 mmol/L** cutoff is supported by the **SugarFACT** RCT (Malawi) — raising it
> did **not** reduce mortality.

touched, pending this answer, so the reference currently prints 3.0 on its malaria and

paediatric-heavy, low-resource, and **a repeat glucose measurement may be delayed or
