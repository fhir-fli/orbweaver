# Cross-book DURATIONS — all 14 candidates read

`tool/crossbook_rules_extract.py` → `rules_side_by_side.md`. **14 candidate groups
across 13 drugs** carried differing course lengths with rows from both books, once
durations were bound to their indication. All 14 read against their pages, and every
one of the 13 drugs is accounted for below. **One finding.** It is Q3 in
`OPENEVIDENCE.md` and appears here only as a record, not as a question.

## 🔴 The finding — quinine loading infusion

The WHO entry's own maximum is **5 mg salt/kg per hour**, and its loading window of
**20 mg salt/kg over 2–4 h** permits **10 mg/kg/h** at the 2-hour end, twice that
maximum, in the same entry. Only the 4-hour end complies. Its maintenance dose is
consistent (10 mg/kg over 2 h = 5 mg/kg/h). The ProtocolBook prints **4 hours flat**,
with no range, which satisfies the cap.

Rapid quinine infusion causes hypotension and arrhythmia, and cardiac monitoring is not
routinely available in the setting these books are written for. Open with OE.

## ✅ Read and NOT a contradiction — reasons recorded so they are not re-raised

- **Acetylcysteine.** Both books give the identical two-bag (200 mg/kg over 4 h then
  100 mg/kg over 16 h, 300 mg/kg over 20 h) and three-bag regimens. The 48 h and 72 h
  rows are **paracetamol ingestion** windows in a toxic-dose table, attributed to
  acetylcysteine because they sit in the NAC section.
- **Amoxicillin.** Pharyngitis 10 d, SAM step-down 5 d, chlamydia in pregnancy 7 d,
  sinusitis 7 d, neonatal severe pneumonia 7 d, uncomplicated SAM 7 d. Six indications.
- **Azithromycin.** A generic 1-then-4-day pack in one book against pertussis
  (day 1 then days 2–5, or 3 days flat) and typhoid (5–7 days) in the other. The 14-day
  row is **erythromycin**, named in the fragment under an azithromycin heading.
- **Ceftriaxone.** Dysentery 3 d, severe dehydration with blood in the stool 3–5 d,
  septicaemia 7–10 d. The 3-day and 3–5-day rows are the same indication and the same
  50–80 mg/kg/day dose, and 3–5 contains 3, so one book is wider rather than different.
  The 7-day row is a terlipressin fragment under a ceftriaxone heading.
- **Ciprofloxacin.** Shigella 3 d against 3–5 d, same 15 mg/kg twice-daily dose, again a
  wider range containing the narrower. Adult uncomplicated UTI 3 d is a different
  population from the 5-day paediatric entry. The 2-day row is a follow-up sentence
  about switching agents after 2 days.
- **Dexamethasone.** Bacterial meningitis 4 days in both books. The 1-day row is
  *"symptoms over 1 day to 9 months"*, a history, not a course.
- **Gentamicin.** SAM 7 days at 7.5 mg/kg in both books, agreeing. The 2-day row is the
  simplified neonatal regimen for when referral is not feasible — gentamicin for 2 days
  plus oral amoxicillin for 7 — which is a different regimen, not a shortened course.
- **Isoniazid.** TB preventive therapy: 3HR for 3 months in both books, 6H for 6 months
  as the alternative where rifampicin fixed-dose combinations are not stocked, 6–9 months
  where ART interactions rule out a rifamycin. All three are on one page and labelled.
- **Metronidazole.** Pelvic inflammatory disease 14 d, bacterial vaginosis 7 d oral or
  5 d intravaginal gel, trichomoniasis 7 d, amoebic persistent diarrhoea 5 d, oral
  infection 7 d.
- **Paracetamol.** The whole group is the acetylcysteine regimen, attributed to
  paracetamol from the words *"paracetamol poisoning"*. Both books identical.
- **Tetracycline.** Purulent conjunctivitis 7 days in measles against 7–10 days in SAM;
  overlapping, and one is eye ointment where the other is drops.
- **Whole blood.** 10 ml/kg over 3 h in SAM without heart failure against 3–4 h in
  severe malaria. Overlapping.

## ⚠ Limits, recorded rather than papered over

- **A heading can lend its drug to a fragment naming a different one.** Four of the 14
  groups contain at least one such row (erythromycin under azithromycin, terlipressin
  under ceftriaxone, acetylcysteine under paracetamol, and the paracetamol ingestion
  windows). The rows are visible and disprove themselves on reading, but they inflate
  the candidate count.
- **A wider range containing a narrower one is flagged as a difference.** 3–5 against 3
  is not a contradiction, and three groups are only that.
- **Indication clustering still merges a page's sub-indications**, the same limit the
  threshold half has.
