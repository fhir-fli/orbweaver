## s9
EN: - If **ANY** concern for residue on skin or clothing — use personal protective equipment (gloves, gowns, masks); remove / bag / discard clothing; wash patient and area thoroughly with soap and water (clothing and PPE are hazardous waste — dispose properly)
- Monitor and support ABCD
- Check vital signs (BP, PR, RR, spO2, Temp)
- If spO2 < 90% or **ANY** concern for respiration, start 100% O2 via facemask
- Bedside RBS and treat hypoglycemia
- Place large-bore IV line
- Perform brief, targeted history and physical exam; pay attention to mental status
  - What was taken, how long ago, volume / how much, immediate effects
- Beware occult trauma
- Obtain labs: CBC, renal functions, electrolytes, liver functions
- 12-lead ECG
- Seizures are a neurological emergency and should be treated promptly with diazepam
- Gastric lavage: almost **NEVER INDICATED**
- Consider **single-dose activated charcoal 1 g/kg PO/NGT within 1 hour** of ingestion, for substances known to be adsorbed. Do not give it to a patient who is not fully awake, and never intubate solely in order to give it

## s13
EN: - See the specific protocol for: Organophosphates / Carbamates / Cholinergics, Hydrocarbons / Paraffin / Kerosene, Benzodiazepines / Opiates, Paracetamol, Ethanol
- **Adrenergic / Sympathomimetics**
  - Cocaine, amphetamines (crystal meth, Adderall), pseudoephedrine
  - Presentation: HTN, tachycardia, mydriasis, anxiety, delirium, delusions
  - Rx: supportive — **benzodiazepines first** (they usually settle the hypertension and tachycardia on their own), plus IV fluids; anti-hypertensives only if still needed
- **Anticholinergic / Tricyclic antidepressants (TCA) / antihistamines**
  - Presentation: hyperpyrexia, cutaneous vasodilation (flushing), decreased saliva, mydriasis, urinary retention, dysrhythmias
  - Rx:
    - Physostigmine — avoid in TCA; only if persistent tachyarrhythmias or convulsions. Call the anesthetist prior to administering.
    - NaHCO3 for TCA
- **Methanol**
  - Presentation: delirium, loss of vision
  - Rx: oral by NGT — 1.8 ml/kg of 43% ethanol (roughly **three 40 ml shots, ≈126 ml**, in a 70 kg adult); maintenance 0.2–0.4 ml/kg/hour of 43% ethanol
  - **Plus folic acid 1 mg/kg (or 50 mg) IV every 4–6 hours** — it speeds conversion of formic acid to CO₂ and water, and is cheap and available
  - **Refer urgently for haemodialysis if available:** pH ≤ 7.15, visual symptoms, seizures, coma, or deterioration despite the antidote. Dialysis clears both methanol and formate and is the definitive treatment
haiku: - Vea el protocolo específico para: Organofosforados / Carbamatos / Colinérgicos, Hidrocarburos / Parafina / Queroseno, Benzodiacepinas / Opiáceos, Paracetamol, Etanol
- **Adrenérgicos / Simpaticomiméticos**
  - Cocaína, anfetaminas (metanfetamina en cristal, Adderall), pseudoefedrina
  - Presentación: HTA, taquicardia, midriasis, ansiedad, delirio, ideas delirantes
  - Tratamiento: de soporte — **benzodiacepinas primero** (habitualmente controlan por sí solas la hipertensión y la taquicardia), más líquidos IV; antihipertensivos solo si todavía son necesarios
- **Anticolinérgicos / Antidepresivos tricíclicos (ADT) / antihistamínicos**
  - Presentación: hiperpirexia, vasodilatación cutánea (rubor), disminución de la saliva, midriasis, retención urinaria, disritmias
  - Tratamiento:
    - Fisostigmina — evitar en ADT; solo si hay taquiarritmias persistentes o convulsiones. Llame al anestesiólogo antes de administrarla.
    - NaHCO3 para ADT
- **Metanol**
  - Presentación: delirio, pérdida de la visión
  - Tratamiento: por vía oral mediante SNG — 1.8 ml/kg de etanol al 43% (aproximadamente **tres tragos de 40 ml, ≈126 ml**, en un adulto de 70 kg); mantenimiento de 0.2–0.4 ml/kg/hora de etanol al 43%
  - **Además, ácido fólico 1 mg/kg (o 50 mg) IV cada 4–6 horas** — acelera la conversión de ácido fórmico a CO₂ y agua, y es barato y está disponible
  - **Derive urgentemente para hemodiálisis si está disponible:** pH ≤ 7.15, síntomas visuales, convulsiones, coma o deterioro a pesar del antídoto. La diálisis elimina tanto el metanol como el formato y es el tratamiento definitivo
