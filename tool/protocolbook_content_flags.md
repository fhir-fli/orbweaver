# ProtocolBook — deferred content/OE flags (from topic headers)

Extracted from each topic file header. Many are author reassurances (already assessed current); the GENUINE open items are distilled below.

## pb_anaemia
- Transfusion thresholds are WHO/LMIC malaria-area-stratified (the book's own scheme); modern restrictive-transfusion practice is broadly consistent. Empirical malaria+sepsis co-treatment and "no way to clinically differentiate malaria from sepsis" is an LMIC-appropriate pearl — kept verbatim.

## pb_anaemia_workup
- "Give all children an antihelminthic" for non-haemolytic anaemia is an LMIC empiric deworming step (helminths → iron-deficiency anaemia); appropriate for the target setting. - Iron 1–2 mg/kg/dose PO q8h = ~3–6 mg/kg/day elemental, standard paediatric therapeutic range. B12 1 mg PO daily (high-dose oral) is a valid alternative to IM where oral is preferred. Kept as printed.

## pb_hydrocarbon
- "Do NOT start prophylactic antibiotics" — matches current evidence (antibiotics only for secondary bacterial pneumonia); steroids likewise not indicated (book doesn't mention). Fine.

## pb_im_acs_treatment
- Aspirin 325 + clopidogrel 300 loading, UFH 60 u/kg bolus (max 4000) + 12 u/kg/hr (max 1000/hr), enoxaparin 30 mg IV then 1 mg/kg SC q12h (hold if CrCl < 30), tenecteplase weight-banded, streptokinase 1.5 M u over 60 min, beta-blocker + ACE-i within 24 h — all standard ACS care. The lytic contraindication list matches ACC/AHA.

## pb_im_agitation
- Verbal de-escalation first, restraints only for imminent harm / failed verbal approach, and haloperidol ± benzodiazepine sedation (benzodiazepine-first for intoxication/withdrawal/undifferentiated, to avoid lowering the seizure threshold / QT issues) is standard. Doses (haloperidol 2.5–5 mg, midazolam 2.5–5 mg IV/IM) are reasonable; monitor airway/respirations after sedation.

## pb_im_aki
- KDIGO-style AKI definition (UOP < 0.5 mL/kg/hr > 6 h; Cr rise ≥ 0.3 mg/dL or ≥ 50%), the classic prerenal urine indices, and the pre/intrinsic/post causal framework are standard. Schistosomiasis as an obstructive cause is an appropriate LMIC-specific addition.

## pb_im_chest_pain
- Aspirin 300/325 mg PO ×1, nitroglycerin 0.4 mg SL q5min ×3, O2 only if SpO2 < 94% (routine O2 no longer recommended in ACS) — all current. HEART (troponin) / TIMI risk stratification is standard; the "use TIMI without labs" fallback is this book's resource-limited adaptation. The Diamond-Forrester-style Likelihood-of-CAD table is the classic pre-test-probability grid.

## pb_im_convulsions
- Benzodiazepine-first (diazepam), then phenobarbital as 2nd line is standard LMIC status-epilepticus practice (where IV levetiracetam/phenytoin may be unavailable). Rectal diazepam 4 × 2.5 mg = 10 mg. Concurrent glucose (50 mL 50% dextrose) + treating the cause is appropriate. Phenobarbital IV load over ≥20 min (respiratory-depression caution) kept.

## pb_im_copd_asthma
- Target SpO2 88–92% in known/suspected COPD to avoid CO2 narcosis is standard (book says 90–92%). Salbutamol/ipratropium q20min ×3, a 5-day prednisolone 40 mg course, and antibiotics for the Anthonisen (increased dyspnoea + sputum volume + purulence) criteria are all current exacerbation care.

## pb_im_dka
- Fixed-rate weight-based insulin (0.1 IU/kg/hr IM or 0.14 IU/kg/hr infusion) after a 1-hour fluid delay, K+ replacement gated on urine output / K < 5.5, the < 50 mL/kg-in-4h cerebral-oedema cap, and corrected-Na-directed fluid choice are all standard LMIC DKA practice (bedside, no blood-gas/osmolality). Metformin-first outpatient titration is appropriate.

## pb_im_dvt
- Wells DVT criteria, risk-stratified D-dimer/ultrasound rule-out, and LMWH/UFH acute anticoagulation are standard. The book bridges to warfarin (10 mg ×2 days → INR 2–3); modern practice favours a DOAC

## pb_im_ecg_basics
- Standard lead placement, calibration (25 mm/s, 10 mm/mV), the 300÷(large boxes) rate rule, sinus-rhythm criteria, normal intervals (PR 0.12–0.20, QRS < 0.12, QTc ≤ 0.44/0.45), and the I/II quadrant axis method are all standard.

## pb_im_ecg_conduction
- RBBB (rSR' in V1, wide slurred S in I/V6) and LBBB (loss of septal r in V1/q in V6, broad monophasic R in V6) criteria are standard. - The book states complete block "QRS 0.10–0.12 sec" and incomplete block "QRS 0.10–0.12 sec" identically for both RBBB and LBBB — as printed the complete/incomplete rows read the same duration range; transcribed faithfully (standard teaching: complete ≥ 0.12 s, incomplete 0.10–0.12 s). Flag for OE.

## pb_im_ecg_enlargement
- Criteria are standard (RAA: peaked P >2.5 mm in II/III/aVF, <0.12 s; LAA: P >0.12 s, notched, biphasic in V1 with terminal negativity >0.04 s or >1 mm; RVH: dominant R in V1, RAD, RV strain; LVH: Sokolow-Lyon SV1+RV5/6 >35 mm, Cornell criteria). All standard.

## pb_im_ecg_ischaemia
- STEMI J-point criteria (V2–V3: men ≥40 ≥2 mm, men <40 ≥2.5 mm, women ≥1.5 mm; ≥1 mm elsewhere) match the 4th-universal-definition thresholds. NSTEMI: horizontal/downsloping ST depression ≥1 mm in ≥2 contiguous leads lasting ≥0.08 s. All standard.

## pb_im_ecg_svt
- Atrial flutter ~300 atrial cycles/min with 2:1 conduction giving a ventricular rate of ~150/min; F waves negative in II/III/aVF and positive in V1; AF irregularly irregular. All standard.

## pb_im_epigastric_pain
- The dangerous differential (ACS, pancreatitis, DKA, cholecystitis, ulcer, pre-eclampsia/HELLP, ectopic), the surgical-consult triggers, the endoscopy alarm features and the test-and-treat vs. scope-first H. pylori age cut-off (55) are all standard dyspepsia guidance.

## pb_im_gi_bleed
- IV PPI (rabeprazole/other), restrictive transfusion (Hb < 7 low- risk / < 9 high-risk), and prophylactic antibiotics (ceftriaxone) for suspected variceal bleeding in cirrhosis are all standard. Whole-blood resuscitation + balloon tamponade as a surgical bridge reflect the LMIC setting. Ranitidine 2nd-line is now withdrawn (NDMA) — an alternate H2-blocker/PPI would substitute; kept as printed and flagged.

## pb_im_headache
- The red-flag set (systemic symptoms, neuro signs, new/thunderclap onset esp. > 40 yo, trauma/toxins, positional/Valsalva-worsened or sleep-waking headache, change in an established pattern) is the standard SNNOOP-style secondary-headache screen.

## pb_im_heart_failure
- The Stevenson profiles + category-directed therapy (diuretic for wet, inotrope/dopamine for cold, ACE-I/beta-blocker/spironolactone for the stable warm-and-dry chronic patient) are standard. Digoxin restricted to proven AF/flutter is appropriate. Morphine in acute pulmonary oedema is now controversial (kept as printed — flag).

## pb_im_hiv_oi
- p.136 toxoplasmosis child leucovorin printed "5 mg PO QD once every 3 days" (contradictory) → rendered "5 mg PO once every 3 days". - CD4 thresholds, TMP-SMX / dapsone prophylaxis, pyrimethamine- sulfadiazine-leucovorin for toxo, ampho+flucytosine → fluconazole for crypto, 21-day TMP-SMX + steroid-if-hypoxic for PCP, and the ganciclovir/valganciclovir CMV options are all standard OI care.

## pb_im_hypertension
- The JNC-8-style essential-HTN targets (≥60: <150/90; <60/DM/CKD: <140/90), thiazide/CCB first-line (ACE-I/ARB for DM/CKD), and the condition-specific acute targets (dissection SBP<120; encephalo- pathy ≤20–25%/hr; avoid nitroprusside in encephalopathy) are standard. Nitroglycerin contraindicated with recent PDE-5

## pb_im_liver_failure
- SBP diagnosis (>250 PMN / ascites+fever+pain) + cefotaxime, non- selective beta-blocker variceal prophylaxis titrated to HR, co-trimoxazole/norfloxacin SBP prophylaxis criteria, and spironolactone±furosemide ascites management are standard. Lactulose for hepatic encephalopathy (after excluding infection/ bleeding/intoxication) is correct. Child-Pugh thresholds standard.

## pb_im_meningitis
- "Steroids no longer recommended" reflects this book's stance; note that adjunctive dexamethasone IS recommended in some adult bacterial-meningitis guidelines (esp. pneumococcal, high-income contraindication logic (raised ICP, coagulopathy, shock) + empiric ceftriaxone/cefotaxime ± ampicillin (>50/immunocompromised, for Listeria) are standard. Meningococcus close-contact prophylaxis (ciprofloxacin / ceftriaxone) is correct.

## pb_im_pe
- PERC (all 8 negative → PE effectively ruled out in low-pretest patients) and the Wells PE criteria/point weights are standard. The book routes definitive anticoagulation through the shared DVT/PE regimen (LMWH/UFH → warfarin); modern practice favours a DOAC.

## pb_im_potassium
prints the range "6.5-6" (reversed) → rendered "K⁺ 6.0–6.5?". membrane stabilisation, insulin-glucose + salbutamol for intracellular shift, and resonium / furosemide / dialysis for elimination are the standard hyperkalemia bundle.

## pb_im_sepsis
- Sepsis-3 definitions (qSOFA, SOFA ≥2, septic-shock lactate/ vasopressor criteria) are current. 30 mL/kg crystalloid bolus, early broad-spectrum antibiotics, and MAP ≥65 vasopressor target are standard Surviving Sepsis guidance; ceftriaxone 2 g first-line is resource-appropriate. Transfuse Hb < 5 is an LMIC threshold.

## pb_im_sodium
- Correction-rate limits (≤ 8 mEq/L/day, 0.5 mEq/L/hr), 3% saline for symptomatic hyponatremia, and the volume-status (hyper/hypo/eu) work-up with U_osm/U_Na and glucose correction are all standard.

## pb_im_sti
- Syndromic management (treat-by-presentation without lab confirmation) is standard WHO practice for low-resource settings. Doxycycline in pregnancy is (correctly) routed to erythromycin; ceftriaxone 250 mg is the older GC dose (now often 500 mg) — flag for OE.

## pb_im_stroke
- LAPSS / ROSIER / ABCD3 / NIHSS are the standard prehospital and bedside stroke instruments. BP target < 170/105 with IV labetalol/ hydralazine reflects a no-thrombolysis LMIC setting (permissive thrombolysis/thrombectomy windows where available.

## pb_im_svt
- Adenosine 6 mg → 12 mg → 12 mg IV push with rapid NS flush, max 3 doses, is standard. ## pb_im_syncope
- Targeted history, orthostatic/exertional/cardiac features, ECG (± ECHO for murmur/exertional/chest-pain syncope), selective labs, and safe discharge if all normal — standard low-risk-syncope workup.

## pb_im_tb
- Standard 2HRZE / 4HR regimen, weight-banded FDCs, pyridoxine 25 mg daily, adjunctive steroids for TB meningitis / pericarditis / airway compression, extended 12-month course for CNS TB, and the ALT-based hepatotoxicity thresholds (hold/alternate if >3–5× ULN) are all standard programmatic TB care.

## pb_mastoiditis
- Mastoiditis is a complication of AOM needing IV antibiotics ± surgical drainage; regimens (Ampicillin-Sulbactam / anti-staph penicillin in children; Ceftriaxone ± Vancomycin in adults for intracranial spread) transcribed as printed.

## pb_neo_cpap

## pb_neo_feeding

## pb_neo_jaundice
