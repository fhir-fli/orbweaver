# ProtocolBook — OPEN QUESTIONS FOR OE

**Nothing is open.** As of 2026-08-28 every question ever raised in this file has been
answered by OpenEvidence and applied. The outcomes are logged in
`protocolbook_needs_oe_rereview.md`; print-side errors are in
`protocolbook_source_errata.md` so they cannot return in v1.8.

Keep this file. When the next audit round raises a question, write it here in the same
shape:

- **Self-contained.** Quote the verbatim current text, state the concern, ask something
  specific. Nothing may refer to a file, a commit, or a previous conversation — OE
  cannot see the repository.
- **State the setting:** district hospital, Gulu, northern Uganda — low-resource,
  paediatric-heavy, malaria-endemic. Limited drug availability; no reliable dialysis;
  ICU monitoring is not routinely available.
- 🛑 **Ask for every figure as a standalone line with no citation after it.** Three
  replies in a row were lost at exactly the number — the citation bracket swallows the
  figure and leaves the sentence reading *"print a ferritin cutoff of [1][2]"* while the
  surrounding prose survives intact. **If it happens again, screenshot the reply instead
  of pasting it.** Images have come through cleanly every time, including tables and
  figures.
- **When a question is answered and applied, DELETE IT from here** and record the
  outcome in `protocolbook_needs_oe_rereview.md`. Sections left sitting here marked
  "answered" are how OE ended up re-answering settled questions twice.
