## The two conflicts are a real decision, not a defect

Both are the word **Dyspnea** (pp.43 and 65). The print points it at **p.6
Breathing**; the app points it at **p.30 Dyspnea**. The print is consistent
about this — it sends symptom words to the ABC universal protocols, the same
way it sends "Hypotension" to p.8 Circulation / Shock — so this is the book's
habit rather than a slip. The app's target is arguably the more useful one for
a reader. Left as the app has it; Grey's call.
